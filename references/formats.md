# Formats

Page types that are not the marketing site, the app, or the shop. Those are `surfaces.md`, `narrative.md`, and `commerce.md`. The same directions and the same tokens apply. Only the job changes.

## Portfolio and case study

The work comes first. A case study opens on the piece or the outcome, not on a portrait and a paragraph about passion.

Structure:

- The name of the work, as the `h1`.
- One sentence on what it was.
- The work, large, with captions.
- Then, if you have them: the constraint, what you did, what changed. Specific. "Cut the checkout from 4 steps to 2. Completion went from 41% to 63%." Not "delivered a seamless experience."
- Next work is one link at the end, not a grid that pulls the eye off the piece before it has been seen.

A portfolio index is a directory (`layout.md`) or a simple list of names and years. The thumbnail is the work, cropped the same way each time. No mockup frames, no device bezels, unless the work is the device UI and the bezel is honest.

## Event and launch

One date, one place or one link, one action. The date is typography, not a calendar widget. A launch page is a poster (`sense.md`) with the facts a person needs to decide: what, when, where, what it costs, how to come.

A schedule is a list: time in `.num`, session name, one line, the room or the link. Not a colored grid of overlapping blocks unless the event is a conference and the overlap is the point. Then it is a tool, and it follows Spec.

Do not count down unless the moment is real and the clock is accurate.

## Help center

A search field with a real label, then the six questions people actually have, as links in a list. Groups are nouns: Billing, Baking, Account. An article is Broadsheet or Civic: a title, the answer in the first paragraph, then the steps. Steps follow `information.md`.

The article does not open with a hero, a smile, and "Welcome to the help center."

## Changelog

Reverse chronological. The date, then a version if you have one, then bullets of what changed in the product's verbs. "You can refund an order from the row." Not "Refund flow improvements and various bug fixes."

Group a day's notes under one date. Do not card each bullet.

## Status

The current state is a sentence at the top: "All systems are up." or "Checkout is down. We are on it." Color helps. The sentence works without it.

Below, a list of parts with a word for each (Up, Degraded, Down) and the time of the last change. An incident is a short log: time, what we know, what we are doing. No marketing.

## Auth-adjacent legal pages

Privacy, terms, and returns are documents. Set them as documents (`surfaces.md` docs): measure, real headings, the date they took effect at the top. Do not design them as marketing pages with illustrations. People came to read the obligation.
